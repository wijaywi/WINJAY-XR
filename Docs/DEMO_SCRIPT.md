 WINJAY XR - Demo Video Script (< 3 minutes)

"One core message:" "Models talk to models - humans watch the state."
Not a chatbot in VR. The Quest is a window into the reasoning state
between agents.

---

 0:00–0:20 - Hook: what you are looking at

-> "This is not chat. You are watching "four AI models talk to each other" -
-> Researcher, Analyzer, Falsifier, Decision - and there is not a single line
-> of chat text for you to read. You are watching their "state"."

- Camera shows passthrough on: the Winjay world floats in the room.
- A hand points at the 4 glowing agent anchors.

 0:20–1:00 - Phase 1: evidence becomes hypothesis

- Pinch "RUN" (or auto-run). Researcher lights up - particles fly:
  "E001, E002" appear as objects (provenance trail).
- Analyzer lights up - sphere "H001" is born in the center, colored CREATED gray.
  "Evidence becomes objects. Hypotheses become nodes."

 1:00–1:40 - Phase 2: confidence rises, then conflict

- H001 turns INVESTIGATING blue → SUPPORTED green.
  Confidence bar "0.62" rises - pinch H001 to open its info card
  (statement + provenance: who did what, in which order).
- Falsifier lights up - "E003" appears orange, a jagged red edge strikes
  H001 (contradiction = physical tension), an "X" marker appears.
  H001 trembles, turns CONFLICTED orange, confidence drops
  "0.62 → 0.42". "Conflict is not an error message - conflict is physical."

 1:40–2:20 - Phase 3: falsification & decision

- H001 FALSIFYING purple (trembling) → FAILED red.
- Decision agent lights up - a decision box drops in: "REJECT_HYPOTHESIS,
  confidence 0.87", with a reason. "Four models negotiate. You only see the
  outcome - the final state."

 2:20–2:50 - Phase 4: second scenario (survival)

- Pinch "SCENARIO" - the world resets. This time the evidence fully
  supports: H001 SUPPORTED → "RESOLVED" gold → "ACCEPT_HYPOTHESIS 0.91".
- "Same state machine, two different futures."

 2:50–3:00 - Closing

-> "WINJAY XR: the Quest as a spatial state machine. You don't read AI
-> conversations - you stand inside them."

---

 Recording tips

- Record with "Meta Quest capture" (in-headset video) for best quality;
  or screen-record the Simulator on PC (OBS).
- One continuous take without cuts = more convincing for a real-time demo.
- Make sure the room is well lit (hand tracking needs light).
- Narration in English; subtitles optional.
